Who We Are

A New Force
for Climate Justice

Founded June 12, 2024

Tour de Force Green Initiative is a dynamic youth-led NGO based in Livingstone, Zambia, founded by climate activist Matongo Miles Munyama — with experience at Oxfam Denmark, Action Aid, and certifications in global organizing and leadership development.

Tour de Force — a French phrase meaning "victory" — reflects our mission to eradicate climate change and promote climate justice by empowering the next generation.

Become a MemberSupport Us
What We Do

Mission & Objectives

We exist to eradicate climate change and promote climate justice by empowering youth across Zambia and beyond.

🌡️
Combat Climate Change

Implement evidence-based strategies to mitigate the effects of climate change at community, national, and global levels.

⚖️
Promote Climate Justice

Ensure fair treatment of all people — especially the most vulnerable — in climate policies and international negotiations.

🎓
Youth Empowerment

Provide comprehensive education and training on climate change and justice for youth aged 16–29 through workshops and mentorship.

🤝
Community Engagement

Assist local communities in adopting sustainable practices that protect both their livelihoods and the natural environment.

📢
Advocacy

Champion policies that support meaningful climate action and justice at every level — local, national, and international.

Programs & Projects

From the ground up — current and upcoming initiatives driving change in Livingstone and beyond.

Active
Green Livingstone Tree Drive

Community-wide tree planting targeting urban heat islands in Livingstone. Working with schools and local councils to plant and maintain indigenous trees.

📍 Livingstone🌳 50+ trees👥 6 schools

Active
Climate Academy

Monthly workshops for youth aged 16–29 covering climate science, advocacy skills, sustainable livelihoods, and leadership development.

📅 Monthly👥 50+ trained📚 8 modules

Ongoing
Community Clean-Up Campaign

Quarterly clean-up drives targeting waterways, parks, and markets — turning awareness into direct environmental action with community volunteers.

📍 3 communities🗓️ Quarterly👥 200+ volunteers

Solar Access for Schools

Helping off-grid rural schools access affordable solar energy — reducing dependence on firewood and improving study conditions for students.

Climate-Smart Farming
